Privacy

What we store

  • Your Telegram user id, chat id, first name and username.
  • Your preferences: roles, cities, experience, whether you want internships, minimum score and daily cap.
  • From your resume, only the extracted skills, years of experience, graduation year and suggested roles, plus a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the file. The resume file and its text are never stored.
  • The alerts we sent you and your 👍/👎 on them.

Gemini (Google's AI)

  • Your resume is read in memory. Emails, phone numbers, links, addresses and the name line are removed first, and only the rest is sent to Gemini to extract skills and experience.
  • Job descriptions from public career pages are sent to Gemini to tag role, seniority, location and skills.

Where data lives

  • The database: Supabase, in Singapore.
  • Short-lived counters (daily alert caps, send rate): Upstash Redis.
  • Events between parts of the app: Aiven Kafka, kept only for its fixed retention (7 days).

How long

  • Alerts: 180 days. Job postings: 60 days after they close.
  • Technical records (processed events, Telegram update ids, check logs): 2 to 30 days.
  • Your account and preferences: until you delete them.

Deleting everything

Send /delete to the bot. It deletes your account, preferences, extracted resume facts, alerts and conversation state at once, plus your counters in Redis, and confirms. Nothing is kept.
